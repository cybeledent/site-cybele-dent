Welcome

A dental practice built for your peace of mind

Cybèle Dent is a dental practice in the heart of Vienne, opposite Saint-Maurice Cathedral. Our two dentists, Dr Céline Filipputti and Dr Laura Agosto, both graduates of the Lyon Faculty of Dentistry, provide comprehensive care for adults, and Clotilde Péaut looks after children aged 3 to 16.

Brand-new, bright premises, a dedicated sterilisation room, panoramic X-ray and 3D cone beam imaging on site, and a team that speaks English: whether you live in the area or are just passing through, you are welcome.

Dental emergencies

Toothache, abscess or broken tooth while in Vienne?

Call us during opening hours: we do our best to see you the same day, even if you are not a registered patient.

📞 During opening hours

Call +33 4 22 97 97 65 and describe your symptoms. We will offer you the earliest possible slot.

🗓️ Sundays and public holidays

Dial 15 (SAMU, the French emergency medical service): they will direct you to the on-call dentist. There is no dental on-call service on Saturdays.

🏥 Warning signs

High fever, swelling of the face or neck, difficulty swallowing or breathing, heavy bleeding: dial 15 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Visitors and expats

Fees and insurance for international patients

The practice is contracted with the French national health insurance (Assurance Maladie), so routine care is charged at the regulated French rates.

🇪🇺 EU, EEA and Swiss visitors

Bring your European Health Insurance Card (EHIC or GHIC): necessary care is provided under the same conditions as for French patients, and we give you the documents to claim at home.

🌍 Visitors from other countries

You pay at the practice (card, cash or cheque) and receive a detailed invoice and treatment report, in English on request, for your travel or health insurance.

📄 Written estimate first

For crowns, implants or any treatment not fully covered, you receive a detailed written estimate before anything starts. Nothing is undertaken without your agreement.

Your questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you speak English?

Yes. Our dentists speak English, so you can explain your symptoms and understand your treatment options with confidence.

I'm visiting Vienne and have a dental emergency. Can you see me?

During opening hours, call +33 4 22 97 97 65: we do our best to see emergencies the same day, whether or not you are a registered patient. On Sundays and public holidays, dial 15 (the French emergency medical service) to be directed to the on-call dentist.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online on Doctolib (choose Dr Céline Filipputti or Dr Laura Agosto for adults, Clotilde Péaut for children aged 3 to 16), or call the practice. Doctolib can be switched to English in its settings.

I'm from the EU. Will my European Health Insurance Card be accepted?

Yes. With a valid European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) or GHIC, necessary treatment is provided under the same conditions as for French patients. We give you the paperwork you need to claim reimbursement from your home insurer.

I'm from outside the EU. How does payment work?

You pay for the treatment at the practice (card, cash or cheque) and we provide a detailed invoice and treatment report, in English on request, so that you can claim from your travel or health insurance.
